Welcome to SKG Chess

SKG Chess is a modern, user-friendly platform designed for chess enthusiasts of all skill levels. Our mission is to provide an engaging and accessible chess experience that brings players together from around the world.

Features

• Real-time multiplayer chess games
• Beautiful, customizable chess boards
• Game statistics and progress tracking
• Interactive tutorials for beginners
• Chat functionality during games

Technology

Built with modern web technologies:
• Spring Boot for the backend
• WebSocket for real-time gameplay
• HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript for the frontend
• Responsive design for all devices

Join Our Community

Connect with chess players worldwide, share strategies, and improve your game. Whether you're a beginner or a grandmaster, there's a place for you in our community.
